### 📹 वीडियो 1: मर्करी (पारा - Hg) — कमरे के ताप पर द्रव धातु
* **विषय:** उच्च पृष्ठ तनाव के कारण गोल बूंदें बनाना तथा कांच को न भिगोना।
* **English Prompt (AI Tool में कॉपी करने हेतु):**
```text
Cinematic 4K macro slow-motion laboratory footage, duration 10 seconds. On a clean dark slate laboratory workbench, a pristine concave transparent glass watch glass holds a dense, gleaming pool of liquid metallic mercury (Hg) with mirror-like chrome reflections. A glass scientific dropper enters from above and releases a perfect silvery mercury droplet. The droplet falls in slow motion, bounces slightly with high surface tension, and seamlessly merges into the convex silvery mercury pool without wetting or sticking to the glass. Small spherical mercury beads roll gracefully around the rim with crisp white studio specular highlights, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, 8k resolution, clean science documentary aesthetic.
```
* **Camera Movement:** Slow downward macro tilt and gentle push-in towards the droplet impact.
* **Negative Prompt:** blur, low resolution, distortion, cartoon, low frame rate, water splashing, dirty table.

Natural skin pores and wrinkles visible in high definition, brilliant metallic reflections on the molten liquid metal, smooth cinematic lighting, extreme macro detail, 60fps slow-motion feel.
